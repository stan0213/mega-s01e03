The two problems

  • parser: every tenant writes the day first, and the shared parser read every date month first. A booking for 7 April came out on 4 July. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed what the hint told them, month first, and the form read it the tenant's way, day first. It only came through the form, on tenants that run booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it. Won't fix: the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket.

The one-day moves

The desk read some reports as a one-day move, not month for day (desk notes). They come from day-first tenants and are counted under parser by tenant, as the footnote under the table shows. A ticket for them on their own was closed into the parser ticket: #3. If one-day moves still come in now that the parser is fixed, they are a problem of their own: check time zones first.

Why it looked like one

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject. Both problems arrived under the same subject lines ("Wrong day on the invite", "Date is wrong"), so the subject alone could never tell them apart.
  • The tag intake puts on a report is a guess from the reporter's browser. The cause comes from the tenant: where they are, which form they run and how the report came in. The "tagged as" column above shows how often the two disagree.

What changes

  • A report merged into #4471 belongs to one of two tickets. Close it with that ticket's outcome, not #4471's.
  • A big ticket can be many bugs under one subject. Split by cause, then count.
